Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 2026

1. Commitment to Privacy

At Terminal Vast Bot, we prioritize the protection and security of your personal data and account privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our platform collects, handles, stores, and protects information when you access our services, web control panel, and WhatsApp automation features.

2. Information We Collect

To provide seamless WhatsApp automation and dashboard services, we collect minimal required information:

  • Account Credentials: Username, email address, hashed passwords, and profile metadata.
  • WhatsApp Session Identifiers: Secure, encrypted session tokens and pairing metadata required for connecting your device to our engine. We do NOT store your WhatsApp plain text password.
  • Service Logs: System operation logs, transaction history, and wallet top-up records required for platform integrity.

3. End-to-End Encryption & Security

Your WhatsApp communication remains encrypted according to WhatsApp standard protocols. Session tokens generated during the pairing process are maintained in isolated storage and protected by standard server-side authorization controls.

4. Data Usage & Protection

Collected information is strictly utilized to operate your bot instance, maintain wallet balance accuracy, deliver customer support, and ensure platform safety against abuse. We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data to third parties.

5. Contact & Support

If you have any questions regarding this Privacy Policy or your personal account data, please connect with us via our official support channel.
